# Pheme commercial licensing

Pheme-owned code may be available under a separate commercial license for
organizations that do not want to comply with GNU GPL v3 when modifying or
distributing that code. No commercial rights are granted by this document. A
commercial license exists only when the relevant copyright holder and the
licensee execute a separate written agreement.

Without such an agreement, the repository is available under GNU GPL v3, whose
text is in [`LICENSE`](LICENSE); the licensing model as a whole is summarized in
[`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md).

## Current scope limitation

A commercial license from the Pheme copyright holder can cover only code for
which that holder has the right to grant commercial terms. Third-party software
and dependencies remain subject to their own licenses.

In particular, parts of the web client are derived from other projects and are
licensed under GNU GPL v3; see [`web/NOTICE.md`](web/NOTICE.md). Pheme does not
have the right to offer those parts under proprietary terms. Consequently, the
current web client cannot be distributed as a closed-source work under a Pheme
commercial license.

A closed-source license for the complete product would first require replacing
the derived portions with independently developed code or obtaining separate 
permission from their copyright holders. Any commercial agreement must also 
identify the included components and applicable third-party terms.

To preserve this model, future contributions must include permission for the
copyright holder to offer them under both GPLv3 and commercial terms, for
example through an appropriate contributor agreement or copyright assignment.
Code accepted only under GPLv3 cannot be included in the commercial edition.

Commercial licensing enquiries should be directed to the project owner.
