Boundary, topographic and construction surveys since 1998

We are a two-person practice working across the county and the three districts adjoining it. Most of our work comes from solicitors, architects and homeowners who need a boundary settled before a sale or a build.

Services

  • Boundary determination and Land Registry plans
  • Topographic and measured building surveys
  • Setting out for construction
  • Party wall award drawings
  • Volumetric and stockpile surveys

How we work

Site work is usually a half or full day. Drawings follow within five working days, issued as PDF and DWG. For boundary disputes we will say plainly when the evidence does not support a clear line — an inconclusive survey honestly reported is worth more than a confident one that will not hold up.
