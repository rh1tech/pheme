# Content rating (IARC questionnaire) — Pheme

Play Console → App content → Content rating.

## Category

**Social networking / Communication** — not a game. Picking the game
questionnaire by mistake produces a different and wrong set of questions.

## Answers

Everything about violence, sexuality, drugs, gambling, crude humour and
frightening content is **No**. Pheme has no content of its own at all; it moves
whatever people send each other.

The questions that matter are the last few, and they are the reason this app
will not be rated for young children:

- **Does the app allow users to interact or exchange content with other users?**
  → **Yes.** Messages, group chats, photos, voice calls.
- **Can users share their current location with other users?** → **No.** Pheme
  has no location feature and requests no location permission.
- **Does the app allow users to purchase digital goods?** → **No.**
- **Does the app contain user-generated content that is not moderated?** →
  **Yes**, in the sense Play means: there is no central moderation queue,
  because the server cannot read the messages. Say so plainly rather than
  hedging.

## Moderation, when asked to describe it

Pheme is end-to-end encrypted, so no operator-side content scanning is possible
by design. Moderation happens at the level of people and membership instead:

- Anyone can leave any conversation.
- A group admin can remove a member.
- A channel owner can ban a subscriber and delete a post.
- The server operator can suspend or delete an account.

That is a defensible position for an encrypted messenger, and it is the same
one every other encrypted messenger on the store takes. What is not defensible
is claiming content is moderated when it cannot be.

## Expected rating

**Teen** (ESRB), **PEGI 12**, **USK 12** or similar. Communication plus
unmoderated user-generated content lands there regardless of how mild the app
itself is.

Do not attempt to get a lower rating by answering No to the interaction
question. It is the one thing the reviewers actually verify, and getting caught
is a policy violation rather than a correction.
