Terms of use

Last updated 1 August 2026

These terms cover the Pheme apps and the instance operated at pheme.rh1.tech. If you signed in to a server run by someone else, your agreement is with that operator.

The software

Pheme is open source and provided free of charge. You may run your own server and use, modify and distribute the code under the licence published with it.

Your account

  • You are responsible for what is sent from your account.
  • Keep your recovery code safe. It is the only way back into your history if you lose every device, and nobody else can reproduce it.
  • You must be old enough to form a binding contract where you live, and at least 13.

Acceptable use

On the instance we operate, do not use Pheme to:

  • break the law, or help somebody else break it;
  • send unsolicited bulk messages;
  • harass, threaten or impersonate people;
  • distribute malware, or attack the service or its users.

We cannot read your messages, so this is enforced on what is reported to us and on what the server can observe — not by inspecting content. We may suspend an account we have good reason to believe is being used for the above.

Availability

The instance we operate comes with no uptime guarantee and may change or stop. That is precisely why the software is self-hostable: if continuity matters to you, run your own and depend on nobody.

No warranty

The software is provided "as is", without warranty of any kind. To the extent the law allows, we are not liable for indirect or consequential loss, including lost messages or lost history.

Pheme is designed so that message history survives losing a device — but it can only protect what a device has actually received and decrypted, and only when a recovery code has been set up. Encryption is a guarantee about who can read your data, not a guarantee that it can never be lost.

Ending it

Delete your account at any time in Settings → Account → Delete account. We may close an account that breaches these terms.

Changes

Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect.
