Privacy policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

Pheme is software, not a service. There is no Pheme account and no central server. You sign in to whichever server runs it, and whoever operates that server decides what happens to your data.

This site does not run one. pheme.rh1.tech serves these pages and nothing else. No accounts exist here and no messages pass through it.

What follows describes what the software collects and what a server running it can see, so that you can judge an instance before trusting it, and so that anyone setting one up knows what they are taking responsibility for. If you use a server run by your company, your community or a friend, that operator is the data controller for your account and their policy is the one that governs it.

What a server running Pheme holds

End-to-end encryption decides who can read content. It does not mean nothing is stored. A Pheme server holds:

DataWhyReadable by the server
Email addressRegistration, sign-in, password resetYes
Display name and usernameSo people can find and recognise youYes
Avatar image, if uploadedShown next to your messagesYes
Message content and attachmentsDelivery and offline storageNo, ciphertext only
Routing metadataWho is in a conversation, timestamps, message sizesYes
Push notification tokenSo notifications can reach the deviceYes
Channel subscriptions and post historyDelivering posts and their backlogPosts are ciphertext; subscriptions are not

Message keys are generated and rotated on the devices themselves using MLS (RFC 9420) and never leave them. An operator cannot read your messages, the contents of your calls or your attachments, and neither can anyone who obtains a copy of the database.

What the software never collects

  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads.
  • No third-party analytics or product telemetry.
  • No crash reporting SDK in the released builds.
  • No contact-list upload. Pheme does not read your address book.
  • No location data.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera, when you take a photo to send or scan a code.
  • Microphone, during a voice call.
  • Notifications, to tell you a message arrived.
  • Photos, for pictures you choose to attach.

Each is requested the first time the feature is used, and the app runs without them.

Push notifications

Where an operator has configured push, delivery runs through Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android and Apple Push Notification service on iOS. Those services receive a device token and the fact that a notification is being sent. They do not receive message content: the payload is not the message, and the app decrypts locally to display it.

How long data is kept

The software stores messages until they are delivered and until you or the other participants delete them. Deleting a conversation removes its stored ciphertext. Account records last until the account is deleted. An operator can set stricter limits and should say so in their own policy.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This asks your server to remove your account record, your profile, your push registration, your channel subscriptions and the message ciphertext it holds for you.

Copies already delivered to other people's devices belong to them and cannot be recalled, the same as any conversation. If you cannot reach the app, contact your operator.

Your rights

Rights of access, correction, export, erasure and objection apply against the operator holding your data, so that is who to write to. Because message content is encrypted with keys they do not have, any export can only contain what their server can actually read.

For questions about the software itself, write to support@rh1.tech.

Children

Pheme is not directed at children under 13.

Security

Traffic between clients and servers, and between federated servers, is encrypted in transit with TLS. Message content is additionally end-to-end encrypted. Pheme has not been independently audited against a security standard. The source is public so that the claims on this site can be checked rather than trusted.
