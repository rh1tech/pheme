Privacy policy

Last updated 1 August 2026

Pheme is an end-to-end encrypted messenger. This policy describes what the software collects and what a server running it can see. It applies to the app and to the instance operated at pheme.rh1.tech.

Pheme can be self-hosted. If you signed in to a server run by your company, your community or another individual, that operator is the data controller for your account and their policy governs it. This document then describes the software's behaviour, not their obligations.

What the server holds

Being end-to-end encrypted decides who can read content. It does not mean nothing is stored. A server running Pheme holds:

DataWhyReadable by the server
Email addressRegistration, sign-in, password resetYes
Display name and usernameSo people can find and recognise youYes
Avatar image, if you upload oneShown next to your messagesYes
Message content and attachmentsDelivery and offline storageNo — ciphertext only
Routing metadataWho is in a conversation, timestamps, message sizesYes
Push notification tokenSo notifications can reach your deviceYes
Channel subscriptions and post historyDelivering channel posts and their backlogPosts are ciphertext; subscriptions are not

Message keys are generated and rotated on your devices using MLS (RFC 9420) and never leave them. The operator of a Pheme server cannot read your messages, your calls' contents, or your attachments, and neither can anyone who obtains a copy of the database.

What it does not collect

  • No advertising identifiers, and no ads.
  • No third-party analytics or product telemetry.
  • No crash reporting SDK in the shipped build.
  • No contact-list upload. Pheme never reads your address book.
  • No location data.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Camera — only when you take a photo to send, or scan a code.
  • Microphone — only during a voice call.
  • Notifications — to tell you a message arrived.
  • Photos — only for pictures you choose to attach.

Each is requested when the feature is first used, and the app works without granting them.

Third parties

Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) delivers push notifications on Android, and Apple Push Notification service does so on iOS. They receive a device token and the fact that a notification is being sent. They do not receive message content — the notification payload is not the message, and the app decrypts locally to show it.

No data is sold, rented, or shared with anyone for their own purposes.

How long it is kept

Messages remain on the server until delivered and until you or the other participants delete them; deleting a conversation removes its stored ciphertext. Account records are kept until the account is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes your account record, your profile, your push registration and your channel subscriptions, and it deletes the message ciphertext the server holds for you.

Copies already delivered to other people's devices are theirs and cannot be recalled — the same as any conversation. If you cannot reach the app, email support@rh1.tech from your registered address.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or erase your personal data, and to object to its processing. Write to support@rh1.tech and we will answer within 30 days. Because message content is encrypted with keys we do not hold, an export can only contain what the server can actually read.

Children

Pheme is not directed at children under 13, and accounts are not knowingly created for them.

Security

Traffic between clients and servers, and between federated servers, is encrypted in transit with TLS. Message content is additionally end-to-end encrypted. Pheme has not been independently audited against a security standard; the source is public so that the claims here can be checked rather than trusted.

Changes

Material changes will be announced in the app before they take effect, and the date at the top of this page will change.
